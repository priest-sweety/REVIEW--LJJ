# REF01 — Single-Nucleus and In Situ RNA-Sequencing Reveal Cell Topographies in the Human Pancreas

- **Citation / DOI:** Tosti L et al. (2021). *Gastroenterology*. DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2020.11.010](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2020.11.010)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Homeostasis & CP
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `EGAS00001004653`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.1 AP; 1.2 CP`
- **Assays and study design:** snRNA-seq (>120,000 nuclei) + RNA-FISH + in situ sequencing in neonatal/adult donor pancreata and chronic pancreatitis
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Defines three human acinar states (Acinar-s secretory, Acinar-i idling/stress-adaptive, Acinar-REG regenerating/REG3A/REG1B+) and shows shift toward Acinar-REG+ dominance with exocrine loss in human CP.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Overcomes hydrolytic RNase degradation in human pancreas; donor cohort sample size and postmortem/surgical covariates must be distinguished from nucleus count.
