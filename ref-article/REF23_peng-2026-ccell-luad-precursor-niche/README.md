# REF23 — Multimodal Spatial-Omics Reveal Co-Evolution of Alveolar Progenitors and Proinflammatory Niches in Progression of Lung Precursor Lesions

- **Citation / DOI:** Peng F, Sinjab A, Dai Y et al. (Wang L, Kadara H) (2026). *Cancer Cell*. DOI: [10.1016/j.ccell.2025.10.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2025.10.004)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research (Cross-Organ Benchmark) · Precursor Epithelial-Inflammatory Co-evolution
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE300288;GSE300293;GSE307534;GSE308103;GSE307529;10.5281/zenodo.15670280`

- **Assays and study design:** Multimodal spatial-omics across 92 human lung precursor/LUAD lesions (486,519 spots; 5.4M cells) + murine anti-IL-1B/ICB precancer interception
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Demonstrates stage-specific epithelial progenitor + IL1Bhigh macrophage proinflammatory niches prevalent in precursors that recede in invasive cancer and respond to early anti-inflammatory interception.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Cross-organ spatial-omics and early inflammatory interception paradigm directly transferable to framing ADM/PanIN-to-PDAC niche co-evolution.
