# REF21 — Single-cell transcriptomics reveals a conserved metaplasia program in pancreatic injury

- **Citation / DOI:** Ma Z, Lytle NK et al. (2022). *Gastroenterology*. DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2021.10.027](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2021.10.027)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Injury-Induced Metaplasia
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE172380`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.1 AP; 1.2 CP; 1.3 PanIN`
- **Assays and study design:** scRNA-seq + electron microscopy across murine pancreatic injury models compared with gastric spasmolytic polypeptide-expressing metaplasia (SPEM)
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Shows injured acinar cells activate a conserved pyloric/SPEM-like metaplasia program generating mucinous, tuft (Dclk1+), and enteroendocrine-like lineages.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Explains cellular origins of chemosensory tuft and gastric pit/spasmolytic lineages that modulate local innate eicosanoid/cytokine signaling.
