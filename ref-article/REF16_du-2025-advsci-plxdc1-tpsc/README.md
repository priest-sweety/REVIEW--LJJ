# REF16 — PLXDC1+ Tumor-Associated Pancreatic Stellate Cells Promote Desmoplastic and Immunosuppressive Niche in Pancreatic Ductal Adenocarcinoma

- **Citation / DOI:** Du Y et al. (2025). *Advanced Science*. DOI: [10.1002/advs.202415756](https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202415756)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · PDAC Stromal-Immune Resistance
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE211895;GSE203612`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.4 PDAC; 2. Immune Reprogramming; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** RNA-seq + ATAC-seq of sorted human PSCs, scRNA-seq, spatial/in situ transcriptomics, and an in-house PDAC immune-checkpoint blockade (ICB) cohort
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Classifies common PSCs differentiating into CCL19+ TPSCs (immune-rich), MYH11+ TPSCs, and hypoxia/stress-induced PLXDC1+ myCAF-like TPSCs driving desmoplasia, immunosuppression, and ICB non-response.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Links epigenetic PSC activation directly to clinical ICB resistance and stromal subpopulation targeting.
