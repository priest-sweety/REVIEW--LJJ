# REF18 — Patient-derived organoids reveal ductal dysfunction and CFTR modulator responses in chronic pancreatitis

- **Citation / DOI:** Osorio-Vasquez V et al. (2026). *Cell Stem Cell*. DOI: [10.1016/j.stem.2026.06.002](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2026.06.002)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Chronic Pancreatitis Ductal Niche & Therapy
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `phs004725;phs001611.v1.p1`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.2 CP; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** Human CP patient-derived ductal organoid biobank, RNA-seq, WGS/WES, and functional forskolin-induced swelling assays with CFTR correctors/potentiators
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Identifies molecular CP ductal subtypes with acquired/genetic CFTR dysfunction and demonstrates functional rescue by clinically approved CFTR modulators.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Major translational anchor for precision epithelial/ductal therapy in CP before irreversible parenchymal loss.
