# REF09 — Single-cell sequencing unveils distinct immune microenvironments with CCR6-CCL20 crosstalk in human chronic pancreatitis

- **Citation / DOI:** Lee B et al. (2022). *Gut*. DOI: [10.1136/gutjnl-2021-324546](https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2021-324546)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Human Chronic Pancreatitis
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE165045`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.2 CP; 2. Immune Reprogramming; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** scRNA-seq + paired TCR-seq + flow/functional assays of human hereditary CP, idiopathic CP, and control donor pancreata
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Uncovers etiology-specific T cell remodeling: CCR6+ CD4+ Th, TNF+ Th1, and Treg enrichment driven by myeloid CCL20 in hereditary CP vs FTH1+ CD4+ and BAG3+ CD8+ T signatures in idiopathic CP.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Human surgical total pancreatectomy samples reflect end-stage intractable CP; sample size per etiology subgroup requires careful weighting.
