# REF19 — Quantitative 3D histology reveals localized immune remodeling during early pancreatic cancer progression

- **Citation / DOI:** Kiemen AL et al. (2026). *Cell Press Blue*. DOI: [10.1016/j.cpblue.2026.100008](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpblue.2026.100008)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · 3D Precancerous Immune Hotspots
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `phs003549.v1.p1`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.3 PanIN; 2. Immune Reprogramming; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** CODA cellular-resolution 3D histology across >1,000 precancers in large human pancreas specimens + spatially resolved DNA-seq
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Reveals dramatic micron-scale spatial heterogeneity around single PanINs, with focal Treg- and macrophage-enriched immunosuppressive hotspots localized to higher-grade dysplasia and ductal obstruction.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Reconciles apparent Treg absence in 2D sections (REF07) by showing focal 3D emergence of immunosuppression primed for early interception.
