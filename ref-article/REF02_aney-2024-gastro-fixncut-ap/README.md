# REF02 — A Novel Approach for Pancreas Transcriptomics Reveals the Cellular Landscape in Homeostasis and Acute Pancreatitis

- **Citation / DOI:** Aney KJ, Jeong WJ et al. (2024). *Gastroenterology*. DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2024.01.043](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2024.01.043)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Homeostasis & AP
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE235874;PRJNA978570`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.1 AP; 2. Immune Reprogramming`
- **Assays and study design:** FixNCut reversible DSP-fixation scRNA-seq + GeoMx Whole Transcriptome Atlas across mouse homeostasis, acute injury (0-24h), ADM peak (72h-5d), and recovery (~14d)
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Captures intact acinar, myeloid, DC (cDC1/cDC2 expansion at 72h), baseline Th2, and IL-33+ fibroblast dynamics across reversible ADM and tissue recovery.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Primary timecourse is murine caerulein AP integrated with human datasets; murine recovery kinetics (~14d) do not directly equate to severe necrotizing human AP.
