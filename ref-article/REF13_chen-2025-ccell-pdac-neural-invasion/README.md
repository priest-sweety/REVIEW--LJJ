# REF13 — Integrated single-cell and spatial transcriptomics uncover distinct cellular subtypes involved in neural invasion in pancreatic cancer

- **Citation / DOI:** Chen MM, Gao Q et al. (2025). *Cancer Cell*. DOI: [10.1016/j.ccell.2025.06.020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ccell.2025.06.020)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · PDAC Perineural Niche
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE278694;HRA008568`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.4 PDAC; 2. Immune Reprogramming; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** Integrated scRNA-seq, snRNA-seq, and spatial transcriptomics across human PDAC tissues stratified by low vs high neural invasion
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Shows non-invaded nerves associate with TLS, whereas invaded nerves are surrounded by NLRP3+ TAMs, myCAFs, TGFBI+ Schwann cells, and basal-like/GABRP+ malignant cells.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Resolves neuro-immune-stromal spatial coupling in PDAC neural invasion and pain/recurrence biology.
