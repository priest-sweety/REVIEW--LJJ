# REF07 — Analysis of Donor Pancreata Defines the Transcriptomic Signature and Microenvironment of Early Neoplastic Lesions

- **Citation / DOI:** Carpenter ES et al. (2023). *Cancer Discovery*. DOI: [10.1158/2159-8290.CD-23-0013](https://doi.org/10.1158/2159-8290.CD-23-0013)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Homeostasis & Sporadic Human PanIN
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE226829;GSE229413;phs002071.v1.p1;10.5281/zenodo.7683038`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.3 PanIN; 2. Immune Reprogramming`
- **Assays and study design:** scRNA-seq + 10x Visium spatial transcriptomics + multiplex IHC across 30 zero-warm-ischemia brain-dead human donor pancreata
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Demonstrates frequent sporadic PanINs in healthy adults, surrounded by C3+/PDGFRA+ fibroblasts, CD163+/SPP1+/APOE+/C1QA+ macrophages, and CD4+/CD8+ T cells with notable scarcity of FOXP3+ Tregs in 2D sampled lesions.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** 2D tissue sampling captures predominantly low-grade sporadic PanINs; contrast with 3D CODA mapping (REF19) showing focal Treg hotspots around higher-grade dysplasia.
