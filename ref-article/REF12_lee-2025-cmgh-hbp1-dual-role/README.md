# REF12 — HMG Box-containing Protein 1 (HBP1) Protects Against Pancreatic Injury in Acute Pancreatitis but Promotes Neoplastic Progression

- **Citation / DOI:** Lee SW et al. (2025). *Cell. Mol. Gastroenterol. Hepatol.*. DOI: [10.1016/j.jcmgh.2025.101536](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcmgh.2025.101536)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · AP-to-PanIN Dual Switch
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `GSE296582`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.1 AP; 1.3 PanIN; 2. Immune Reprogramming`
- **Assays and study design:** Pancreas-specific conditional Hbp1 KO mice (+/- KrasG12D), human pancreatitis tissues, spatial transcriptomics, and multiplex protein profiling
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Demonstrates stage-dependent duality of transcription factor HBP1: cytoprotective during acute pancreatitis injury, yet promoting early PanIN progression in the presence of oncogenic Kras.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Key example of context-dependent transcriptional switches where anti-injury programs become co-opted for neoplasia.
