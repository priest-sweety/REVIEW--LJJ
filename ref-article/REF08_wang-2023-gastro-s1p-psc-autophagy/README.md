# REF08 — Pancreatic Acinar Cells-Derived Sphingosine-1-Phosphate Contributes to Fibrosis of Chronic Pancreatitis via Inducing Autophagy and Activation of Pancreatic Stellate Cells

- **Citation / DOI:** Wang D et al. (2023). *Gastroenterology*. DOI: [10.1053/j.gastro.2023.08.029](https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2023.08.029)
- **Article type & disease stage:** Original Research · Chronic Pancreatitis Fibrosis
- **Public datasets / code accessions:** `NOT_REPORTED_IN_TEXT`
- **Mapped review sections:** `1.2 CP; 2. Immune Reprogramming; 3. Therapeutic Windows`
- **Assays and study design:** Caerulein and pancreatic duct ligation mouse CP models, human CP IHC/IF, and injured acinar cell (iPAC)-PSC coculture assays
- **Key findings for pancreatic inflammatory niche synthesis:** Establishes that injured acinar cells upregulate SPHK1 and secrete S1P, engaging S1PR2 on PSCs to trigger autophagy-dependent PSC activation and fibrogenesis.
- **Limitations / transferability notes:** Directly resolves how persistent acinar injury initiates stromal activation in CP and nominates SPHK1/S1P/S1PR2 as an anti-fibrotic target.
